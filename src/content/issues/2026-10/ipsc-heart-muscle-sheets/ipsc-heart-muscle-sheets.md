Japanese surgeons transplanted heart-muscle cell sheets derived from induced pluripotent stem cells (iPSCs) into a patient with ischemic heart disease. The treatment aims to improve cardiac function, but larger studies and long-term follow-up are needed to establish its effectiveness.
