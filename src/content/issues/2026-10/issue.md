The release of this issue marks the beginning of the arw,sc newsletter. We extend our thanks to all authors for their contributions and our readers for joining us at the start of this journey. Congratulations are also in order for the participants from Science Club at the NBHRC 2026 for their achievements and for representing the club.

The lead story for this issue tackles the looming future and prospects of robots entering the surgical field. Daraxonrasib makes its debut in the battle against pancreatic cancer, while optogenetics receives renewed recognition following this year’s Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine. Two articles break down how the gut exerts influence over multiple systems in the body. Dengue is in the news again, but it has a new face. And lastly, an opinion piece on the part of medicine that is beyond the books.
