In a first-in-human trial, the experimental inhaled siRNA therapy ARO-RAGE was well tolerated and reduced biomarkers indicating successful targeting of RAGE in the lungs. It is an early-stage investigational treatment – not a replacement for existing asthma inhalers – and its clinical benefits remain unproven.

**Read more:** [Nature Medicine](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-026-04607-z)

**Source:** O’Carroll M, Kasahara D, Huetsch J, et al. Inhaled siRNA therapy targeting RAGE for pulmonary inflammation: a first-in-human randomized trial. *Nat Med*. 2026;32:3339–3350. [doi:10.1038/s41591-026-04607-z](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41591-026-04607-z)
