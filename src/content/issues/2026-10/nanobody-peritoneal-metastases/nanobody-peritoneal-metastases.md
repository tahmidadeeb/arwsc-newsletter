Singaporean researchers identified cancer-promoting proteins in ascitic fluid and developed a nanobody, E8, targeting PAI-1. It suppressed tumour growth in laboratory, animal and human-tissue models, but it has not yet been proven safe or effective in clinical trials.

**Read more:** [National Cancer Centre Singapore](https://www.nccs.com.sg/news/research/sg-research-team-develops-novel-nanobody-targeting-tumour-fuelling-fluid-to-treat-late-stage-cancer-of-the-abdominal-lining)

**Source:** Liu Y, Chong CYL, Wang Y, et al. Identification of novel paracrine therapeutic targets in peritoneal metastases through integrative proteotranscriptomic analysis of ascites. *Mol Cancer*. 2026;25:225. [doi:10.1186/s12943-026-02787-3](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12943-026-02787-3)
