A clinical trial involving more than 1,100 patients found that the personalized mRNA vaccine intismeran, combined with pembrolizumab, reduced the relative risk of melanoma recurrence or death by 42% compared with pembrolizumab alone. The vaccine is investigational, and longer follow-up is needed to establish the durability of its benefits.
