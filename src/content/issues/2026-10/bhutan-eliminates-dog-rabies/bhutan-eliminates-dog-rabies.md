The WHO validated Bhutan’s elimination of dog-mediated human rabies as a public-health problem, making it the first country in the WHO South-East Asia Region to achieve this milestone. Sustained dog vaccination, surveillance, post-exposure prophylaxis and community engagement were central to its success.
